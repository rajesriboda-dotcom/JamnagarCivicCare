Citizen Service Guarantee Charter

Under the Gujarat Right to Public Services Act, Jamnagar Municipal Corporation guarantees prompt acknowledgement and strict SLA-backed action for all civic concerns:

Guaranteed Tracking

Unique reference ID with SMS acknowledgement sent instantly upon complaint lodging.

Fixed SLA Timeline

Streetlight faults within 24 hrs, water leaks within 24-48 hrs, and garbage cleared within 12 hrs.

Direct Escalation

Unresolved tickets automatically escalate to Zonal Deputy Municipal Commissioner.
